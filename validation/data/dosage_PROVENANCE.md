# Per-gene dosage response of TCGA breast tumours — provenance

Generated by `validation/data/build_dosage_tcga.py` on Arrhenius (NAISS), 2026-11-13, and read by
`validation/paper/supp_dosage.py`, which fits iscc's dosage-sensitivity prior to it.

## Sources
1. **TCGA PanCanAtlas** (open access; https://gdc.cancer.gov/about-data/publications/pancanatlas):
   - `EBPlusPlusAdjustPANCAN_IlluminaHiSeq_RNASeqV2.geneExp.tsv` (`api.gdc.cancer.gov/data/3586c0da-64d0-4b74-a449-5ff4d9136611`), batch-corrected RSEM expression;
   - `TCGA_mastercalls.abs_segtabs.fixed.txt` (`.../0f4f5701-7b61-41ae-bda9-2805d1ca9781`), ABSOLUTE allele-specific integer copy number;
   - `TCGA_mastercalls.abs_tables_JSedit.fixed.txt` (`.../4f277128-f793-4354-a13d-30cc7fe9f6b5`), ABSOLUTE purity and ploidy;
   - `merged_sample_quality_annotations.tsv` (`.../1a7d7be8-675d-4e60-a105-19d4121bdebf`), cancer type and `Do_not_use`.
2. **GENCODE v19** gene annotation (hg19, matching the ABSOLUTE coordinates):
   `https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/gencode/Gencode_human/release_19/gencode.v19.annotation.gtf.gz`.

The raw files are not redistributed; their sha256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS` beside them on the cluster
(`/nobackup/proj/disk/naiss2025-22-1610/personal/pedrof/iscc/data/tcga_pancan`).

## Samples and genes
BRCA primary tumours (sample code 01) with an ABSOLUTE call, RNA, and no `Do_not_use` flag: 1,020,
of which 315 have purity at least 0.7. Autosomal genes of GENCODE v19 whose symbol is unique, with at
least 100 samples: 17,413.

## Outputs
- `dosage_tcga_brca.csv.gz`, one row per gene: the slopes (and standard errors) of log2(expression + 1)
  on copy number, all tumours with a purity-adjusted covariate (`beta*`) and high-purity tumours with
  log2(copy number / ploidy) (`beta_hp*`), each for all changes, gains only and losses only; and the
  same regression on expression generated by iscc's two dosage laws at `s` on a grid (`h*`: the
  "haplotype" law, `k*`: the "total" law; `m*`: the haplotype law under the all-tumour covariate).
- `dosage_tcga_brca_summary.json`: sample counts, purity and ploidy medians, the grid.
- `dosage_fit.json` (from `supp_dosage.py`): the fitted priors per law and the induced slope summaries.
